## Causal-clock and failure-accounting audit

The report generator `research/polymarket-5m/forward/latency.py` does not implement
the current receipt-causal execution contract:

1. Spot triggers are indexed by exchange `trade_ts` and books by exchange
   `source_ts`. Local `receive_ts` is retained, but it is not the availability
   gate for the legacy strategy decision.
2. A trade row triggers at its exchange timestamp and the simulated match book is
   selected at exchange timestamp plus the configured lag. Therefore the reported
   300/400/500 ms is not demonstrated as local receipt → decision → send → venue
   wait → match time.
3. The book-health check requires an update after the candidate match time. The
   close-crossing check also searches later receipts to decide whether a disconnect
   occurred. Both use information unavailable at decision time.
4. Candidates failing those later health checks are dropped from the sample. They
   are not retained as submitted attempts with a zero fill or explicit send/failure
   outcome. This can condition the reported fill and PnL sample on future health.

Those behaviors violate the frozen research requirements that every feature and book
be available by local receipt time, health decisions use only information known at
decision time, and every attempted send/zero fill/partial fill/failure be preserved.
The separate V3 receipt-clock engine is not the engine that produced these legacy
reports.

## Other rank7 gaps

Even without the causal-clock defect, these reports do not establish:

- one identical, continuous and complete seven-UTC-day BTC5m window with all 2,016
  scheduled markets and official outcomes;
- one independent $1,000 cash ledger per strategy/scenario with 100-share targets,
  no daily reset, no borrowing and settlement-to-spendability timing;
- the 500 ms main scenario plus separately pinned 600/1,000 ms, half-depth and
  extra-one-cent stresses;
- exact-size 100-share execution evidence rather than displayed L1 capacity or
  modeled fill availability.

CLOB-BTC and Binance-strict coarse heartbeats for run 39435891649 did not show the
long RTDS writer-enqueue gaps noted elsewhere, so this rejection does not depend on
the RTDS outage. It rests on the report generator's source-clock selection,
post-decision filtering, failure accounting, and rank7 coverage/account mismatch.

## Required repair and evidence class

A future candidate must be generated by code frozen before a strictly later untouched
window and must prove, by immutable input/code/scenario hashes:

- local-receipt availability for spot events and book updates;
- decision-time-only data-health gates;
- latency measured from local decision through actual modeled match without double
  counting venue delay;
- explicit attempted, rejected, unfilled and partially filled orders;
- complete rank7 coverage and the independent 100-share/$1,000 cash contract.

Changing the schema or engine now cannot retroactively validate Oct10/11 or the
Oct12–19 protocol. A repaired engine requires a later untouched registration window.
